Privacy

A plain-language summary of what Peakform collects and why.

What we collect

Your account email and password (stored using Django's standard password hashing — we never see or store it in plain text), and the profile details you choose to enter: age, height, weight, sex (only when your chosen calorie formula requires it), activity level, experience level, goals, available equipment, dietary preferences, and units. These power your personalized nutrition and workout targets, and you can edit or leave most of them blank.

What you log

Food diary entries, water and step logs, workout sessions and exercise logs, recovery notes, and anything you choose to post to the community feed (including photos you upload) are stored so you can track progress over time. Community posts, comments, and your username are visible to other users and, for the public feed, to visitors who are not signed in.

AI assistant

If you use natural-language food logging, the text you type is sent to Google's Gemini API to estimate nutrition values. Nothing is logged to your diary automatically — you review and confirm each estimate first.

What we don't do

We don't sell your data, and we don't share it with third parties beyond the service providers needed to run the app (hosting, database, and the Gemini API when you use the AI assistant).

Your controls

You can edit your profile at any time, delete your own posts and comments, and unfollow anyone you follow. Contact your site administrator to request deletion of your account.
